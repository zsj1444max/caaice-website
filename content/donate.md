Decisions being made right now — in Johor, in Jakarta, in Singapore — will determine how Asia's data centres are powered for the next twenty years. Those decisions are being made quickly, with limited public scrutiny, and with very few independent voices at the table.

We are trying to be one of them.

## What your support funds

CAAICE runs on a simple model: we research questions that matter, and we publish the answers where anyone can read them. No paywall, no subscription, no sponsored conclusions.

Your gift funds:

- **Original research** into power procurement rules, cross-border renewable trade and grid access across Southeast Asia
- **Public education** that translates technical energy policy into terms families, journalists and local officials can use
- **Cross-regional dialogue** connecting policymakers and researchers across California and Asia

We are a small organisation. Gifts of any size change what we are able to take on this year.

## Ways to give

**Make a gift**

Online giving is being set up. In the meantime, please write to [info@caaice.org](mailto:info@caaice.org) and we will send you everything you need, including a receipt for your records.

**Give in a child's name**

Through our Climate Baby program, you can make a gift in a child's name. We will send a Growth Map placing that child's milestones alongside the milestones of Asia's energy transition — a reminder of what these decisions are actually about.

**Partner with us**

Foundations, universities and research institutions interested in collaboration are welcome to write to us directly.

## Our commitments to you

**Independent.** Funders do not review, approve or influence our research conclusions.

**Transparent.** We publish all sources of funding.

**Open.** All research is published free of charge and freely reusable.

These are not aspirations. They are the terms on which we accept support.
